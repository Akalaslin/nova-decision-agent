FAQ

Good questions

What is NOVA?

An AI assistant for decisions. It remembers your goals, limits, past decisions and documents, and uses them to give answers you can check, and plans you can follow.

Is it free?

You can try NOVA free. A Pro plan with more messages and a stronger model is planned.

Can I trust the answers?

Every answer shows the memories and document parts it used, and a Guard step checks that the sources it cites were really found. NOVA can still be wrong, so treat it as a thinking partner, not a final authority.

Does NOVA give medical, legal or financial advice?

No. It helps you think a decision through, and it points you to a qualified professional for those areas.

Is my data private?

The public demo uses sample data only. Please don't type personal or confidential information: the AI service behind it is on a free tier, where the provider may use inputs to improve its products.

Why is the demo read-only?

The public demo uses sample data and doesn't save changes, so it stays safe and free. AI use is limited per visitor and per day.
